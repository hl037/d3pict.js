Class: D3PProbe ​

Defined in: core/probe.ts:82

What is under the pointer on one chart: the service tooltips, cursors and markers read.

It answers from a D3PPointerEvent, which is the cache: the value along an axis and the hit of a renderer are computed when someone first asks, and once. An event may come from another chart (the tooltip and the cursor follow the pointer across charts); the axes then match by name, by join, or by sharing a domain.

The probe also drives the markers of its renderers: as soon as a renderer is attached, the pointer events of the chart show and hide them.
