D3PictDepict your data.

SVG charts you build from objects, on top of d3. Interactive, animated, and yours to extend, in plain TypeScript, Vue or React.

Get started
See the showcase
GitHub

  • Five objects make a chart

    A series, two axes, a renderer, a chart. Swap the renderer and a line becomes bars, a heatmap or a pie. Everything else is one more object you add, or leave out.

  • Polar is a change of words

    Turn `left` into `radius` and `bottom` into `angle`: the same series, renderer and tooltip now draw a polar chart. Charts whose axes share a domain zoom together, polar or not.

  • New data just moves

    Assign `series.data` and the points glide to their new place. Each point is matched with the one that has the same x, so nothing swaps places.

  • No private parts

    The legend, the titles and the axis titles are made with the pieces you get. Replace one, or write your own the same way.

  • Plain TypeScript, Vue or React

    The same objects everywhere. `useD3PContext()` makes them in a component and destroys them with it.

  • A file when you need one

    Save a chart as an SVG or a PNG, with its fonts, its title and its legend. The cursor and the tooltip stay out of it.
